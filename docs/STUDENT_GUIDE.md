# Student guide

Register, log in and complete enrollment with CNIC, college and MBBS year. Select an available package, pay using the configured instructions, then submit the transaction reference and proof. Access remains unavailable until the administrator approves payment. A rejected submission shows the reason and allows corrected proof without deleting earlier records.

With active access, practice by subject/module/topic, resume sessions, bookmark questions and revise mistakes. Practice reveals explanations after answering. Timed exams reveal answers after submission; refreshing does not reset the deadline. Results and progress use your saved attempts. Expired or suspended access blocks BCQs while retaining profile and payment access. Use the visible Log out button when finished.

To leave the platform, open **My profile → Delete account**, enter your current password and confirm. Your account and associated student records are permanently removed and you are signed out. The confirmation lists the data removed. This action cannot be undone.
